# Date picker validation

The date picker opens from the main header and presents month navigation plus selectable day buttons. Selecting the future date **2026-09-01** updated the picker label and the task-input placeholder to that selected date, confirming that new tasks are targeted at the chosen future day.

The task **「準備下週專案簡報」** was added after selecting 2026-09-01. The list rendered a separate **2026年9月1日週二** section after the existing 2026年8月27日週四 group, confirming chronological, date-based grouping for future task entries.
